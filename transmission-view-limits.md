---

One way to think about a patient message is as a chain: the doctor and your medical record hold the information, the AI turns it into a message, the patient portal delivers it, and you read it. Along the way, mistakes, made-up details, or confusing medical terms can slip in. But even a perfectly accurate message can still fail. 

You might finish reading it and feel confused, scared, or brushed off. Good healthcare communication depends on trust, feelings, and real understanding, not just getting facts from one place to another. If we only ask whether the information arrived correctly, we miss most of what makes a message from your doctor truly helpful.
